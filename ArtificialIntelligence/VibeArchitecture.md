# Vibe Architecture
In this activity, try to think about _what_ software you might be able to build if you could build _anything_. You are not limited by your skills, only your imagination!

## Part One: Blue Sky Brainstorming (15m)
Think about what you might want to make. The only criteria is that it has to be software. Utilize the [blue sky thinking](https://drawn.agency/our-thoughts/blue-sky-thinking-what-it-and-why-do-we-use-it/) technique - basically, there are **no bad ideas**. You may find later that there certainly _are_ bad ideas, but to start, pretend there could never be a bad idea.

Here are some software ideas:

- A game
- A social media app
- A personal website
- An immersive art piece

## Part Two: Engineering (15m)
The next part is where skills are required. It may be tempting to just say "hey ChatGPT build my idea" but that's not always going to work!

![](./Assets/vibe-coding-vs-vibe-engineering-v0-vw0d41sbvnse1.webp)

Luckily, A.I. is pretty good at figuring out _how_ to do something if it knows _what_ it's doing very specifically.

1. Go to [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com)
1. Give it your ideas
1. Ask it about **feasibility**
1. Ask it about **frameworks**
1. Ask it about **architecture**
1. Ask it about **tools**
1. Ask it to teach you about these things
1. Ask it how you can get started

Note that none of this is Vibe Coding, because it does not involve code at all - it's more like creating plans / blueprints.

## Part Three: Implementation (15m)
Now for the part where you try to actually bring something into existence. This is where it gets especially tricky, because you may have to download / install software or actually get something up and running. You _might_ be able to host your project in [HyTOP](https://hytop.onrender.com/), but most software applications require a bit more than just front-end HTML/CSS/JS or front-end Python.

That said, ask ChatGPT to give you some code, and see what happens! If things aren't working, go back and ask how to fix them.

### Side-note: Context
Context is _extremely_ important when speaking with chatbots. In fact, since LLMs are basically just giant auto-completes, the context _you_ provide does a lot to determine output. Some important items to provide:

- Your experience level with different technologies
- Your goals in building your piece of software / learning
- The capabilities of HyTOP and your current computer
- The software you have downloaded / installed

## Conclusion
Hopefully, by the end of this activity, you will have some idea of the power AND the limitations of an A.I. software development workflow. You might even have something cool to show, even if it's just a prototype or one piece of something larger!
